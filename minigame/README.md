A modern, interactive, and visually stunning implementation of the classic Rock Paper Scissors game with advanced features and competitive gameplay.

## 🎯 Overview

Rock Paper Scissors Arena transforms the traditional hand game into an engaging digital experience with animations, statistics, and multiple gameplay modes. Battle against an AI opponent in a race to 5 wins!

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 **Core Gameplay**
- Classic Rock Paper Scissors mechanics
- First-to-5 wins format
- Real-time score tracking
- Round history and statistics

### 🎨 **Visual Experience**
- Animated choices with visual feedback
- Dynamic arena with weapon selection
- Winner celebration animations
- Dark/light theme toggle
- Responsive design for all devices

### 🏆 **Advanced Features**
- **Auto Play Mode**: Watch AI vs AI battles
- **Hint System**: Get strategic advice
- **Win Streak Tracking**: Track consecutive victories
- **Game Statistics**: Comprehensive player stats
- **Sound Effects**: Immersive audio feedback
- **Local Storage**: Save game progress and history

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Prerequisites
- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- No installation required - runs directly in browser

### How to Play
1. **Open the game** by launching `index.html` in your browser
2. **Choose your weapon**: Click Rock, Paper, or Scissors
3. **Battle**: Watch the computer make its choice
4. **Win rounds**: First to 5 wins claims victory
5. **Use special features**: Try Auto Play or get hints
